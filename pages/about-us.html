Our Story

Built for Maharashtra's Font Lovers

MarathiFontStyle.co.in was built because finding quality Marathi fonts online was needlessly complicated. Most tools required signup, charged money, or showed only a handful of fonts.

We built the tool we wished existed — fast, free, comprehensive, and designed specifically for Marathi and Devanagari typography. No fluff. No paywalls. Just fonts.

Marathi uses the same Devanagari script as Hindi, giving you access to 60+ high-quality Google Fonts with no cost, no signup, and no installation.
