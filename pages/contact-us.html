What to Expect When You Reach Out

We read every message that comes through this form personally — there's no auto-responder pretending to be a real reply, and no ticket queue that messages disappear into. Most enquiries get a genuine response within one to two business days, though bug reports involving a specific font rendering issue sometimes take a little longer if we need to test the fix across multiple browsers and devices before confirming it's resolved.

Bug Reports — What Helps Us Fix Things Faster

If you've spotted a Marathi conjunct letter rendering incorrectly, a font that isn't loading, or a converter producing wrong output, the fastest way to get it fixed is including specifics: which font or tool, the exact text you typed (copy-paste it rather than describing it), and which device and browser you were using. A screenshot helps enormously too. Font rendering bugs can be surprisingly device-specific — something that looks broken on one phone's browser sometimes displays perfectly on a laptop — so details narrow down the cause much faster than a general "this font looks wrong" note.

Font Requests

We're regularly adding new Devanagari fonts to the generator, and requests genuinely shape what gets added next. If there's a specific font you've seen used elsewhere — on a poster, another website, a printed document — that you'd like to see in our generator, let us know the font name if you have it, or describe the style (bold display, delicate script, formal serif) and where you saw it. We check every suggestion against Google Fonts' library for proper Devanagari and Marathi conjunct support before adding it.

Business and Partnership Enquiries

For advertising, partnership, licensing questions, or any business-related enquiry, select "Business Enquiry" in the subject dropdown above so it reaches the right person directly rather than sitting in a general support queue. Please include as much relevant detail as possible in your first message — the type of partnership or enquiry, any relevant numbers or timelines — since it helps us give you a useful response on the first reply rather than a round of clarifying questions.

Other Ways to Reach Us

Email remains the most reliable way to reach us for anything detailed or requiring a considered response. For quick, casual feedback — a typo you noticed, a small suggestion — the contact form above works just as well. We don't currently offer live chat or phone support, since a small independent site like this one can respond more thoroughly and thoughtfully to email than to real-time chat, especially for technical font issues that need actual testing before we can give you a confident answer.

Reporting a Font Licensing Concern

Every font featured in our generator is sourced from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License or Apache License 2.0, both of which permit free personal and commercial use. If you believe a specific font is listed incorrectly, has a licensing issue we've overlooked, or you represent a font foundry with a concern about how your typeface is presented on our site, please reach out using the contact form with as much detail as possible, and we'll review and respond promptly. We take licensing accuracy seriously, since it affects everyone who relies on this site for legitimate commercial use of these fonts.

Feedback on the Redesign and New Features

This site is actively maintained and regularly updated — new fonts, new converter tools, and design improvements are added on an ongoing basis based directly on user feedback. If something about the layout, navigation, or a specific tool feels confusing or could work better, we genuinely want to hear it. Small, specific pieces of feedback ("the Copy button on mobile is hard to tap" or "I wish the pagination showed more styles per page") are often more useful to us than general praise or general complaints, since they point to something concrete we can actually act on.

For Educators, Students, and Researchers

We occasionally hear from students working on linguistics or typography projects, and teachers looking to use our tools as a classroom resource for Marathi-medium schools. We're happy to answer specific questions about how our converters work technically, or to point you toward the Google Fonts documentation for a particular typeface's design background, if that's useful for academic work. Mention your context in the message and we'll do our best to help.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a response?

Most messages get a reply within one to two business days. Complex bug reports that need cross-device testing occasionally take a bit longer, but we'll always acknowledge receipt of your message.

Can I request a custom font be added to the generator?

Yes — use the contact form with "Font Request" as the subject, and include the font name or a description of the style you're looking for. We check every request against Google Fonts for proper Marathi support before adding it.

Do you offer paid or priority support?

No — every user gets the same response quality and timeline regardless of the nature of their message, in keeping with our commitment to keeping this site free and equally accessible to everyone.
