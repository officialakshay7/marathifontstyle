Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MarathiFontStyle.co.in ("we," "us," "our," or "the site") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, including our font generator, Unicode converter, and Kruti Dev converter tools. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below. If you have any questions after reading this, please contact us.

Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. Specifically:

  • Text you type into our tools — the font generator, Unicode converter, and Kruti Dev converter all run entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. The text you type or paste is never transmitted to our servers, never stored, and never logged. It exists only in your browser's memory for as long as the page is open.
  • Standard analytics data — through Google Analytics, we collect anonymised, aggregated information such as pages visited, approximate location (country/city level), device type, and browser, to understand how the site is used and where to improve it.
  • Contact form submissions — if you use our contact form, we receive the name, email address, and message you provide, solely to respond to your enquiry.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies for two main purposes: enabling Google Analytics to measure site usage, and enabling Google AdSense (and other third-party advertising partners) to serve and measure advertisements. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, though some site functionality (particularly ad-supported features) may not work as intended without them.

Google AdSense and Advertising

This site displays advertisements through Google AdSense and, on some pages, additional third-party advertising networks. These advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services that may interest you. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings page, or through the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page for participating networks generally.

Third-Party Services We Use

We rely on a small number of third-party services to operate this site, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Google Fonts — font files are loaded from Google's font delivery network to display Marathi and Devanagari typography correctly.
  • Google Analytics — for anonymised, aggregated traffic and usage insights.
  • Google AdSense and other ad networks — to display advertising that supports the free operation of this site.

Each of these third parties operates under their own privacy policy, and we encourage you to review Google's privacy policy for details on how they handle data collected through Analytics, Fonts, and AdSense.

Data Retention

Since our core tools process text entirely client-side, we retain no record of the content you type or convert. Analytics data is retained in aggregated, anonymised form according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings. Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to resolve your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterward for reference, unless you request earlier deletion.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold about you (primarily limited to contact form submissions, since our tools don't store typed content). To exercise any of these rights, contact us directly and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information through our contact form, please reach out and we will remove it promptly.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect any information we do collect (such as contact form submissions) from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

How We Use the Information We Collect

The limited information we do collect is used strictly for operating and improving the site: aggregated analytics data helps us understand which tools and articles are most useful so we can prioritise improvements and fix problems faster; contact form information is used solely to respond to your specific enquiry and is not added to any marketing list or shared with third parties beyond what's necessary to respond to you. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to any third party for their own marketing purposes.

International Users

MarathiFontStyle.co.in is based in Maharashtra, India, and primarily serves users in India, though the site is accessible globally. If you're accessing this site from outside India, please be aware that any information collected (such as through the contact form) may be processed and stored on servers located in different jurisdictions, including through our third-party service providers like Google, which operates infrastructure across multiple countries. By using this site, you consent to this general data handling approach.

Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting that signals your preference not to be tracked across websites. Currently, there is no consistent industry standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals, and our site does not alter its data collection practices in response to DNT browser settings at this time. You can still control tracking through your browser's cookie settings and the ad opt-out links provided in the AdSense section above.

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out through our Contact page. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within a reasonable timeframe.
